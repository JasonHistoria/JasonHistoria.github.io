I am an incoming MS student in Computer Science at [UC San Diego](https://cse.ucsd.edu/), where I will work with [Xiaolong Wang](https://xiaolonw.github.io/) on robot learning. I recently received my BS in Computer Science from the [Paul G. Allen School](https://www.cs.washington.edu/) at the University of Washington.

I work on **robot learning** — in particular world models and vision-language-action policies that let a single model handle many robots, many sensors, and many compute budgets. My first paper, [Flex-π](https://flex-pi.github.io), is a multi-stream world-action model whose speed–accuracy operating point is chosen at deployment rather than baked in at training time.

I am always happy to talk about robot learning. Email is the best way to reach me.
