I am an incoming MS student in Computer Science at [UC San Diego](https://cse.ucsd.edu/), where I will work with [Xiaolong Wang](https://xiaolonw.github.io/) on robot learning. I recently received my BS degree in Computer Science from the [Paul G. Allen School](https://www.cs.washington.edu/) at the University of Washington.

My research interests are in **robot learning** and **foundation models** — in particular how a single model can generalize across many robots, many sensors, and many compute budgets.

I am always happy to talk about research. Email is the best way to reach me.
