Jinghao Liu

Incoming MS in Computer Science, UC San Diego · BS in Computer Science, UW Allen School

I am an incoming MS student in Computer Science at UC San Diego, where I will work with Xiaolong Wang on robot learning. I recently received my BS in Computer Science from the Paul G. Allen School at the University of Washington.

I work on robot learning — in particular world models and vision-language-action policies that let a single model handle many robots, many sensors, and many compute budgets. My first paper, Flex-π, is a multi-stream world-action model whose speed–accuracy operating point is chosen at deployment rather than baked in at training time.

I am always happy to talk about robot learning. Email is the best way to reach me.
